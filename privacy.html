Privacy

We keep this simple.

  • We count page visits and link clicks with privacy-friendly analytics that don't set cookies or identify you.
  • We don't run ad trackers.
  • Nyla, our chat helper, runs in your browser. What you type isn't sent or saved anywhere; she only remembers when she last said hello, so she doesn't pop up on every page.
  • Our fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when a page loads.
  • If you click through to a booking site, that site has its own privacy policy and may set its own cookies.
  • If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

Questions: privacy@nynightlife.com.
