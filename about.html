About

NY Nightlife is a small, independent guide to getting into New York bars and clubs: what to wear, how old you need to be, whether there's a cover, and how the door works.

It's run by Dan Herlehy. Research and drafting are done with AI tools; every fact is tied to a source you can click, and Dan reviews changes before they go live. Pages that haven't had that review yet say "editor review pending".

We don't take payment for coverage. Some links may earn us a commission; those are labeled. See affiliate disclosure and how we check.
