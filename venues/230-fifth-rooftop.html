Short answer

230 Fifth Rooftop is a rooftop bar in NoMad / Flatiron, Manhattan. Dress code: Casual, no sweats or gym clothes. Cover: $15 Fri / $20 Sat from 8pm; $30 skip-the-line tickets on Eventbrite; no cover other nights. Hours: Mon–Thu noon–1:30am (last order); Fri noon–late (last entry 2am); Sat 11am–4am (last entry 2:30am); Sun 11am–1:30am (last order). Official site.
