NoMad / Flatiron · rooftop bar

230 Fifth Rooftop

Dress code
Casual, no sweats or gym clothes

Official info page says to wear what you love but no sweatpants or gym clothes; a third-party listing reports a stricter weekend code (no athletic wear, hoodies, ripped or baggy jeans).

Skip it if

  • You hate crowds: reviewers say the rooftop gets packed on summer evenings and Fri–Sat primetime.
  • You want a quiet drink late on weekends: after 8pm it shifts to a cover-charge, line-at-the-door party scene.
