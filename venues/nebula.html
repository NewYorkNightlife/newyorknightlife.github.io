Midtown / Times Square · club

Nebula

Dress code
Upscale; enforced at the door

The official SpeakeasyGo event page bans sports attire, shorts, tank tops or sleeveless shirts, sweatpants, flip-flops and slides for men. For women it bans flip-flops, slides, sneakers, sandals and open-toe shoes, and encourages dresses and heels. Admission is at door staff's discretion.
