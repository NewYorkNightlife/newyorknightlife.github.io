Nebula — Midtown / Times Square

Status: Open — official site lists events this week.

Last verified: 2026-08-25 — every fact below is sourced; anything we couldn't confirm is marked as reported or unverified.

The facts

Address135 W 41st St, New York, NY 10036
NeighborhoodMidtown / Times Square, Manhattan
Type11,000 sq ft big-room nightclub (~800 capacity, 35 bottle-service tables)
MusicEDM and house headliners Friday–Saturday; “Nebula Tuesdays” weekly party leans open-format
Nights & hoursTuesday, Friday, Saturday — 11pm–4am
Cover / entry costGA tickets roughly $20–$60 by event, sold via Speakeasy (linked from the official site)
Dress codeUpscale and enforced (reported): collared shirts/dress shoes for men; no athletic wear, shorts, jerseys, or distressed sneakers
Age policy21+
Tables & reservationsVIP reservations via the official site; birthday packages available; minimums not published (third-party quotes ~$1,000–$2,000+ — unverified)
Peak timesFriday and Saturday headliner nights
Official sitenebulanewyork.com
Instagram@nebulanewyork

How to get in

Buy tickets through the official site's Speakeasy links. The door favors dressed-up groups; arrive before midnight on headliner nights.

The honest take

The biggest club to open in Midtown in years — a polished, LED-heavy, Vegas-style room near Times Square. Draws tourists, birthday crews, and EDM fans; strict door and premium bottle pricing are the recurring themes.
