How to get in

  1. Pick your night: Nebula runs Tuesday, Friday, and Saturday, 11pm–4am. Fridays and Saturdays are the headliner nights; “Nebula Tuesdays” leans open-format.
  2. Buy GA in advance through the official site's Speakeasy ticket links — roughly $20–$60 depending on the event.
  3. Dress up — the code is upscale and enforced: collared shirts and dress shoes for men; no athletic wear, shorts, jerseys, or distressed sneakers.
  4. Arrive before midnight on headliner nights — the door favors dressed-up groups and the line builds fast.
  5. You must be 21+ with valid ID.
  6. For VIP: reserve tables via the official site (birthday packages available). Minimums aren't published — third-party quotes of ~$1,000–$2,000+ are unverified, so confirm when booking.

The honest take

The biggest club to open in Midtown in years — a polished, LED-heavy, Vegas-style room near Times Square. Draws tourists, birthday crews, and EDM fans; strict door and premium bottle pricing are the recurring themes.

Getting there & getting home

Nebula is at 135 W 41st St, steps from Times Square — probably the best-connected club address in America. Times Sq–42nd St (N, Q, R, W, S, 1, 2, 3, 7) and 42nd St–Bryant Park (B, D, F, M) are both within two blocks, and the A, C, E at Port Authority are close behind. All of it runs 24 hours, so the 4am ride home is a short walk to a train — with the usual overnight waits. Rideshare pickups around Times Square are chaotic at club closing; walking a block or two off the main drag gets you a faster, cheaper car.
