Knockdown Center — Maspeth

Status: Open — 50 upcoming shows listed through 2027 (Underworld, Honey Dijon, Flying Lotus, Sasha).

How to get in

  1. Find your event — programming is event-driven, concentrated Thursday–Sunday, from daytime outdoor Ruins shows (often starting 3pm) to late club nights.
  2. Buy on Dice, early — all tickets go through Dice (official FAQ), big names sell out, and tiered presales are cheaper before the price jumps. Typical range is roughly $25–$70; no refunds except lineup changes.
  3. Check the age restriction on your event page before buying — most events are 21+, always noted per event, and valid ID is always required.
  4. Pack light: there's no re-entry unless specified, and lockers are available to rent inside.
  5. Doors typically open 90 minutes to 2 hours before showtime (reported); for club shows, headliners often play after midnight.
  6. Sort your ride home in advance — this is Maspeth, and the venue's own FAQ calls it a 20-minute walk from the Jefferson St L.

The honest take

One of NYC's best big-room experiences — multiple stages, serious bookings, plenty of bar capacity. Real downsides: it's a schlep (a 20-minute walk from the Jefferson L per the venue's own FAQ), acoustics get mixed reviews for live-band shows, and sold-out mega-raves mean long lines and a packed floor.

Getting there & getting home

Knockdown Center is at 52-19 Flushing Ave in Maspeth, Queens — by the venue's own FAQ, about a 20-minute walk from the Jefferson St stop on the L, which runs 24 hours. The Q54 and Q59 buses run along Flushing/Grand Ave nearby, and the B57 and B38 connect from Brooklyn. Realistically, most people arrive and leave by rideshare — budget for it, and expect surge right at closing when a sold-out show empties. If you do walk the L route late, go in a group; it's an industrial stretch with little foot traffic.

FAQ

How much are tickets at Knockdown Center?

All tickets are sold via Dice (official FAQ). The typical range is roughly $25–$70 depending on the act, with tiered presales cheaper — current listings from around $43. No refunds except lineup changes.

Is Knockdown Center 21+?

It varies by event — most events are 21+, and the restriction is always noted on the event page. Valid ID is always required.

Is there re-entry at Knockdown Center?

No re-entry unless specified for the event. Lockers are available to rent, so pack light.

How do you get to Knockdown Center?

It's a 20-minute walk from the Jefferson St L stop (per the venue's own FAQ), or the Q54/Q59/B57/B38 buses. Most people rideshare.

What kind of music does Knockdown Center book?

House, techno, and electronic marquee bookings (Underworld, Sasha, Honey Dijon, Joy Orbison, Horse Meat Disco) plus live rock, hip-hop, and indie.

What is Basement at Knockdown Center?

The complex also houses Basement, its strict no-phones techno club — a separate room with its own events and door culture.
