The Dead Rabbit

The official FAQ gives no dress code; reviews describe a busy, come-as-you-are pub downstairs.

Age
21+ in the Parlor; under-21s are allowed in the Taproom before 9pm only with a parent or guardian, seated at a table
Cover
None listed (some live-music nights are ticketed via Eventbrite/Resy)1 source
Getting in
Reservations on Resy recommended, especially for the Parlor; walk-ins always kept. Taproom bar seats are first come, first served. Table limits are 90 min for walk-ins and 2 hr for reservations.
Hours
Taproom: Sun–Thu 11am–2am, Fri–Sat 11am–3am. Parlor: Tue–Sat 5pm–1am, Sun 4pm–12am, closed Mon.
Drinks
Cocktails ~$18–25; a 20% service charge on the bill has been reported1 source
Nearest subway
Whitehall St (R/W), ~3 min walk; South Ferry (1) and Bowling Green (4/5) are also close (estimated from the address)1 source
