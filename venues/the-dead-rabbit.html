How to get in

  1. Decide which floor you want — they're different nights. The Taproom downstairs is the fast, loud Irish pub; the Parlor upstairs is the refined, seated cocktail room.
  2. For the Parlor, book on Resy the moment the window opens (reported bookable 14 days out) — prime slots go fast. Parlor hours: Tue–Sat 5pm–1am, Sun 4pm–12am, closed Monday.
  3. For the Taproom, just walk in — but avoid the FiDi after-work rush Thursday/Friday 5–8pm if you want a table. Walk-ins take the bar.
  4. Time the happy hour for value: Sun–Thu 3–6pm and 11pm–2am, with $8 Guinness pints and $2 oysters (reported, June 2026). Standard cocktails run in the high teens.
  5. No dress code — come-as-you-are downstairs; smart-casual is typical for the Parlor (reported).
  6. It's 21+ to drink; the pub serves food from 11am, but the minors policy is unverified — ask the venue if it matters to your group.

The honest take

Deservedly legendary — arguably the best Irish pub and one of the best cocktail programs in America under one roof. Downsides: perpetually mobbed, the Taproom gets shoulder-to-shoulder and loud, and it sits in a corner of FiDi that's dead quiet otherwise, so it's a destination trip for most people.

Getting there & getting home

The Dead Rabbit is at 30 Water St in the Financial District, a corner of Manhattan that's dead quiet at night except for this bar. Whitehall St (R, W), South Ferry (1), and Bowling Green (4, 5) are all within a few minutes' walk, and the Staten Island Ferry terminal is around the corner — this is one of the few world-class bars you can reach by free boat. Trains run 24/7, but overnight frequencies down here are sparse; if you close the Taproom at 2–3am, a rideshare north is often the realistic move.

FAQ

Do you need a reservation at The Dead Rabbit?

The Taproom is walk-in friendly (walk-ins take the bar). The Parlor books via Resy — reportedly 14 days out — and prime slots go fast.

What are The Dead Rabbit's hours?

Taproom: Sun–Thu 11am–2am, Fri–Sat 11am–3am. Parlor: Tue–Sat 5pm–1am, Sun 4pm–12am, closed Monday (official, fetched 2026-08-25).

Does The Dead Rabbit have a happy hour?

Yes — Sun–Thu 3–6pm and 11pm–2am, with $8 Guinness pints and $2 oysters (reported, June 2026).

Is there a dress code at The Dead Rabbit?

Nothing official — come-as-you-are in the Taproom; smart-casual is typical for the Parlor (reported).

What's the difference between the Taproom and the Parlor?

The Taproom is the fast, loud Irish pub downstairs; the Parlor is the refined, seated cocktail room upstairs — same building, two different nights.

When is The Dead Rabbit busiest?

The FiDi after-work rush Thursday/Friday 5–8pm, and tourist-heavy weekend afternoons. Prime Parlor seatings book out fast (reported).
