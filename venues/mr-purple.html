Mr. Purple

Dress code
Upscale / smart casual

The official site asks guests to follow its attire guidelines and dress in an 'upscale', fashion-forward way. The Rooftop Guide says smart casual, with no flip-flops, sportswear or torn clothes. The IHG and OpenTable listings say casual, probably meaning daytime dining.
