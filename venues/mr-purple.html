How to get in

Walk in early or book via SevenRooms. After 10pm Friday–Saturday expect a line and standing room only unless you reserve a table.

The honest take

The LES scene rooftop — relaxed and great-value by day, young and packed by weekend night, when it's long lines and standing unless you buy bottles.
