Le Bain — Meatpacking District

Status: Open — official site shows current summer hours and this week's events.

Last verified: 2026-08-25 — every fact below is sourced; anything we couldn't confirm is marked as reported or unverified.

The facts

Address444 W 13th St (The Standard, High Line penthouse), New York, NY 10014
NeighborhoodMeatpacking District, Manhattan
TypePenthouse discothèque + rooftop bar (with the famous sunken hot tub)
MusicHouse and disco-leaning; serious DJ residencies (Eli Escobar Wednesdays, Susanne Bartsch Thursdays, François K Sundays)
Nights & hoursMon–Tue 4pm–12am; Wed–Fri 4pm–4am; Sat 2pm–4am; Sun 2pm–12am (summer 2026 schedule; day sessions on weekends)
Cover / entry costNo cover — the venue states in writing there's no cover for standing room and bar service. Tables optional with minimum spends
Dress codeDress-to-impress; no sportswear, jerseys, or hats; discretionary door that gets harder late (reported — not published officially)
Age policy21+ (reported)
Tables & reservationsOptional tables with minimum spends, booked through Resy
Peak timesWednesday–Saturday from 10pm for club nights; weekend sunsets for the rooftop
Official sitelebainnewyork.com
Instagram@lebainnyc

How to get in

No reservation needed for the floor — but the weekend door is selective and lines get long after 11pm. Tables via Resy. Sunset sessions from 2–4pm on weekends are the easy way in.

The honest take

The most credible actual club on Manhattan's rooftop circuit — real DJ bookings, a fashion-y, queer-friendly crowd, and free entry traded against a hard, arbitrary door. The hot tub is real.
