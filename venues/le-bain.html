Le Bain — Meatpacking District

Status: Open — official site shows current summer hours and this week's events.

Last verified: 2026-08-25 — every fact below is sourced; anything we couldn't confirm is marked as reported or unverified.

How to get in

  1. Decide your mode: club night (Wednesday–Saturday from 10pm) or the easier rooftop day session (weekends from 2pm — the low-stress way in).
  2. No reservation is needed for the floor, and the venue states in writing there's no cover for standing room and bar service.
  3. Dress to impress: no sportswear, jerseys, or hats (reported — not published). The door is discretionary and gets harder as the night goes on.
  4. Go before 11pm on weekends — after that, lines get long and the door gets pickier.
  5. 21+ (reported); bring ID.
  6. Want certainty? Book an optional table with a minimum spend through Resy — a reservation converts the discretionary door into a confirmed entry.

The honest take

The most credible actual club on Manhattan's rooftop circuit — real DJ bookings, a fashion-y, queer-friendly crowd, and free entry traded against a hard, arbitrary door. The hot tub is real.

Getting there & getting home

Le Bain crowns The Standard, High Line at 444 W 13th St in the Meatpacking District. The A, C, E, and L at 14th St–8th Ave are about five minutes away, and all four run 24 hours — as direct a subway link as Manhattan clubbing gets. After close, 13th St and 9th Ave are thick with rideshares; Meatpacking's cobblestones are also walkable to the West Village if you want a wind-down bar. Sunday–Tuesday the venue closes at midnight, so the late-late transit question only really applies Wednesday–Saturday.

FAQ

Does Le Bain have a cover charge?

No — the venue states in writing there's no cover for standing room and bar service. Tables with minimum spends are optional.

What is the dress code at Le Bain?

Dress-to-impress: no sportswear, jerseys, or hats (reported — not officially published). The discretionary door gets harder late.

Is Le Bain 21+?

21+ (reported). Bring ID.

What are Le Bain's hours?

Summer 2026 schedule: Mon–Tue 4pm–12am; Wed–Fri 4pm–4am; Sat 2pm–4am; Sun 2pm–12am, with weekend day sessions.

Is the hot tub at Le Bain real?

Yes — the famous sunken hot tub on the penthouse floor is real.

What music does Le Bain play?

House and disco-leaning, with serious residencies: Eli Escobar on Wednesdays, Susanne Bartsch on Thursdays, and François K on Sundays.
