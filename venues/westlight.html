Short answer

Westlight is a rooftop bar in Williamsburg, Brooklyn. Dress code: Casual (nothing official). Cover: None listed; tables may carry a food-and-drink minimum. Hours: Mon–Thu 4pm–12am; Fri 4pm–1am; Sat 12pm–2am; Sun 12pm–12am. Reserve on Tablz via the official site. Facts checked 2026-10-18.
