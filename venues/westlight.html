How to get in

Book via the official site (SevenRooms) — sunset and weekend slots go fast. Walk-ins queue for the elevator at peak.

The honest take

Routinely rated among NYC's best rooftop views — a 360° Manhattan panorama from the 22nd floor. It's a cocktail bar, not a party; the costs are premium drinks and sunset crowds.
