Getting in
Book on Resy; reservations reportedly open 7 days ahead and fill fast. You enter through the phone booth inside Crif Dogs. There's no standing room, so walk-ins may be turned away or told to come back. Even with a booking, expect to wait at Crif Dogs.

Skip it if

  • You're a group or want to drop in spontaneously: it's tiny, has no standing room, and walk-ins are often turned away.
