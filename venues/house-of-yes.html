How to get in

Buy advance tickets via the official calendar (events frequently sell out). Door tickets only if not sold out. No photos on the dance floor — enforced. Strong consent culture (“Safer Spaces” policy).

The honest take

NYC's flagship costume-party club — aerialists over the dance floor, themed nights, and a crowd that commits. Expressive and queer-friendly; not a bottle-service scene. Book ahead: the good nights sell out.
