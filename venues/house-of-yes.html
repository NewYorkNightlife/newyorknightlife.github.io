Short answer

House of Yes is a club in Bushwick, Brooklyn. Dress code: No formal code; costumes encouraged. Cover: Ticketed per event; Dirty Circus GA ~$43, cabaret table for 2 ~$77; party tickets tiered and rise closer to the date. Questions

What is the dress code at House of Yes?

No formal code; costumes encouraged. The official FAQ says Friday and Saturday parties are themed and to 'dress to express'; other nights, wear what makes you feel fun and fabulous. Time Out notes guests are known for elaborate costumes.
