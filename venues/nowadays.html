Ridgewood · club

Nowadays

Dress code
None published

Nowadays publishes no dress code. Its Safer Space rules cover behaviour instead: zero tolerance for discrimination, ask before touching, no staring, and no phones or photos on or near the dance floor.

Cover
Outdoors free except Sundays (Mister Sunday is ticketed). Night parties usually around $25. If the cover is a barrier, email ahead for free or reduced entry.
Getting in
RSVP or buy tickets on Resident Advisor; the outdoor bar and yard are walk-in
Hours
Outdoors: Thu 5pm-12am, Fri 5pm-3am, Sat 2pm-3am, Sun 3pm-9pm (Mister Sunday, ticketed). Indoors: Fri 10pm until the party ends (usually 6am); Sat 10pm straight through to Sun 10pm during Nonstop.1 source

Not found yet
Nearest subway
Halsey St (L). Walk down Wyckoff Ave toward the train bridge, then turn right onto Cooper Ave; about 5-10 min. Also the B26 bus to Irving and Halsey.
Accessibility
The indoor wheelchair entrance is via the driveway; during parties the gate is locked, so ask staff at the street entrance. One accessible stall indoors (ask staff for the key) and one accessible bathroom outdoors. All bathrooms are gender-neutral. The back yard is rough dirt and gravel, and the outdoor dance floor is soft and slightly uneven. Strobes and haze at most night parties. Water and earplugs are free.
