Nowadays — Ridgewood

Status: Open — official site lists current weekly hours and an active calendar.

How to get in

  1. Pick your session — this venue runs several distinct ones: outdoor Thursdays/Fridays/Saturdays, indoor club nights Friday and Saturday, Mister Sunday outdoors on Sundays (ticketed), and Nonstop — the 24-hour party from Saturday 10pm straight through Sunday.
  2. Buy RA tickets in advance for Friday/Saturday nights and Mister Sunday — walkups get turned away when sold out. Covers vary but tend to be around $25 (official guidance).
  3. If cover is a barrier, the sliding scale is real and official: email hello@nowadays.nyc a few days ahead for free or reduced entry.
  4. Nighttime parties are 21+ (reported via RA listings); bring ID. Dress is casual — there's no code.
  5. At the door you'll get a short briefing on consent and phone rules. Take it seriously: no filming or photos on the dance floor.
  6. There's no bottle service and no tables — this is a room for dancing, with free water and earplugs inside.

The honest take

The best-run dance space in NYC for actually dancing — strict no-phones and no-photos rules on the floor, great sound, free water and earplugs. Downsides: the etiquette rules read as preachy to some, covers plus fees add up, Friday/Saturday sells out, and the yard is rough dirt and gravel underfoot.

Getting there & getting home

Nowadays is at 56-06 Cooper Ave in Ridgewood, Queens, near the Bushwick border. The L at Halsey St is the usual train, followed by a roughly 15-minute walk; the venue is also reachable via nearby bus lines along Myrtle and Cooper. The L runs 24 hours, which matters for the indoor nights that run to ~6am — and for Nonstop, which simply doesn't end. Rideshares are the common exit after 3am; the walk back to the L is doable in groups. If you're coming for Mister Sunday in the afternoon, daylight makes the whole trip easy.

FAQ

How much is cover at Nowadays?

Official guidance: covers vary but tend to be around $25, with tickets/RSVP via RA. An official sliding scale exists — email hello@nowadays.nyc a few days ahead if cover is a barrier, and free or reduced entry is offered.

Is Nowadays 21+?

Nighttime parties are 21+ (reported via RA listings); daytime outdoor sessions are more open (unverified).

Can you take photos at Nowadays?

Not on the dance floor — the no-phones/no-photos rule is strict, and the door briefs everyone on it along with the consent policy.

What is Nonstop at Nowadays?

The 24-hour party: it starts Saturday at 10pm and runs continuously through Sunday 10pm indoors.

What is Mister Sunday?

The flagship outdoor Sunday party from the Mister Saturday Night crew (Justin Carter & Eamon Harkin), ticketed, Sundays 3pm–9pm in season.

Does Nowadays have bottle service?

No — no tables, no bottle service. Free water and earplugs are provided; private event inquiries go to events@nowadays.nyc.
