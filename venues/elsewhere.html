Elsewhere

Age
16+ to 21+ depending on event; under-16 never admitted. Scannable US government photo ID or passport required (internationals: passport only); no expired IDs or photocopies
Cover
Ticketed per event; Oct 2026 listings ran about $10–36, with some headliner tiers up to ~$100. Door tickets after the event starts have no fees1 source
Getting in
Buy tickets on Eventbrite or at the door; bags inspected, mandatory bag check at big events. Zone One/Loft/Rooftop use the left courtyard entrance; Hall uses the right corridor entrance
Hours
Varies by event; usually closes around midnight Mon–Thu, 4am Fri–Sat, 11pm Sun
Drinks
Not found yet
Nearest subway
L to Jefferson St (~5 min walk) or Morgan Ave (~10 min); B57 bus stops at Flushing/Johnson Aves
Accessibility
Fully wheelchair accessible including the rooftop; some elevator areas need a staff escort. Reserved seating can be arranged via info@elsewhere.club or security at the door

Skip it if

  • You dislike lines and crowds: reviewers advise queuing early for weekend rooftop parties, and the Hall holds about 600.
  • You want a polished cocktail experience: it is a loud, large warehouse venue with drinks served in plastic cups.
