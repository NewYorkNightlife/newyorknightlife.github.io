Pacha New York — East Williamsburg

Status: Open (reopened June 2026) — formerly Avant Gardner / Brooklyn Mirage, rebuilt and relaunched under the Pacha brand. Reported to be operating on temporary permits as of summer 2026; September events are on sale.

Last verified: 2026-08-25 — every fact below is sourced; anything we couldn't confirm is marked as reported or unverified.

How to get in

  1. Check the calendar at pacha-nyc.com — events run mostly Thursday–Sunday, 10pm–4am, with the open-air main stage in season roughly June–October.
  2. Buy GA presale on the official site (“no hidden fees”). GA runs $50–$130 depending on the headliner, and Saturday headline shows sell out first — the Aug 29, 2026 show sold out both GA and VIP.
  3. Want VIP? “Backstage” packages are $175–$850 per person via vip.pacha-nyc.com, with pricing published per event.
  4. Check the age line on your specific event page — headline events have been 21+, and it isn't centrally published. Bring a physical ID.
  5. Plan transport before you go: the venue runs a dedicated shuttle — check the site for current details.
  6. Skip resale sites entirely, and know that anything sold as “Brooklyn Mirage tickets” is not real — that venue no longer exists.

The honest take

The Brooklyn Mirage's massive open-air successor, relaunched under the Ibiza Pacha brand after Avant Gardner's 2025 bankruptcy. Premium pricing, huge production, heavy demand — and a venue still proving itself to regulators and the neighborhood after the Mirage-era chaos.

Getting there & getting home

Pacha New York is at 140 Stewart Ave in East Williamsburg, an industrial stretch about a 15-minute walk from the L train at Jefferson St or Morgan Ave. The L runs all night, which matters at 4am, but the walk is dark and industrial — groups tend to walk it together or split a short rideshare to the station. The venue also runs its own dedicated shuttle (check pacha-nyc.com for the current route). At closing, expect surge pricing on rideshares as thousands leave at once; walking a few blocks before calling a car helps.

FAQ

How much are tickets at Pacha New York?

GA tickets run $50–$130 depending on the headliner, listed directly on the official site with “no hidden fees.” “Backstage” VIP runs $175–$850 per person.

Is Pacha New York the old Brooklyn Mirage?

Same address, new venue: after Avant Gardner's 2025 bankruptcy, FIVE Holdings (owner of Ibiza's Pacha brand) rebuilt and relaunched the site as Pacha New York in June 2026.

Is Pacha New York 21+?

Headline events have been 21+, but age policy isn't centrally published — check your specific event page and bring a valid physical ID.

What nights is Pacha New York open?

Event-driven, mostly Thursday–Sunday, 10pm–4am. The open-air main stage season runs roughly June–October; the indoor Great Hall runs beyond it.

Is there a dress code at Pacha New York?

Nothing is published. Mirage-era crowds were casual/rave attire, but that's unverified for the Pacha era — when in doubt, casual and comfortable is the norm for this kind of big-room show.

How do you book a table at Pacha New York?

Via “Book Backstage” at vip.pacha-nyc.com, which publishes per-person pricing ($175–$850). Full table minimums aren't published.
