Pacha New York

Dress code
No beachwear; VIP is stricter

Brooklyn Magazine (Apr 2026) quoted the club's site: no beachwear (swimsuits, flip-flops, men's vests, bare torsos). The VIP area also bans shorts, sportswear, sneakers, sweatshirts and caps. The current official contact/FAQ page doesn't show a dress code.

Cover
Ticketed. GA from ~$40-145 for most Oct-Nov 2026 nights (one headliner from $450). Backstage/VIP from ~$150-450. Late early-entry arrivals pay the difference to the door price.
Getting in
Buy tickets in advance on the official site; big headliner nights sell out. Backstage/VIP is booked on the site too. Staff run security checks at the door, and gum was confiscated on opening night.1 source
Hours
Event nights only. Most events run 10pm-4am (the open-air closing ran 8pm-1am). Check each event.

Skip it if

  • You want cheap or spontaneous: it's ticketed, with prices that climb by tier, and the biggest nights sell out.
  • You're after a small, intimate club: it's a mega-venue (Billboard estimated ~5,000 on the floor on opening night).
