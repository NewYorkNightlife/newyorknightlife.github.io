How to get in

Reserve via the Weekly Events page — strongly recommended, bookable 30 days out. Entry backs up after midnight on weekends; the pool deck is daytime programming in season.

The honest take

Instagram-bait sky-high rooftop — garden décor, skyline views, a pool — that draws a dressed-up date-night and birthday crowd more than serious dancers. Gorgeous views; strict door, pricey drinks, and post-midnight entry backups are the trade.
