Somewhere Nowhere

Dress code
Smart casual, strictly enforced

The official FAQ calls nightlife 'smart casual' and bans shorts, athletic wear, team logos, pool slides/flip-flops, distressed sneakers and tank tops; shorts are allowed only at Daylife events.

Age
21+ (government ID required)
Cover
Ticketed: GA (entry before 1am) or Bar Reservation (entry + 4 drink tickets, before 3am); prices vary by event and aren't published1 source
Getting in
Buy GA or a bar reservation via the venue's Weekly Events page (opens 30 days ahead, non-refundable); bottle service by email or phone. Late-night entry after 12:30am depends on capacity. Some dinner and show packages are on OpenTable.
Hours
Wed 5pm–1am; Thu closed; Fri–Sun 12pm–4am (official; events vary)
Drinks
Very expensive ($$$$). Minimum spends are common: about $50–100 per person for seated and show bookings. Martini Power Hour specials are $12, 5–7pm (reported).1 source
Nearest subway
23 St (F/M) at 6th Ave, ~4 min walk; 28 St (1) at 7th Ave, ~5 min (estimated from the address)1 source
Accessibility
On the 38th–39th floors of a hotel, reached by elevator; no accessibility policy is published1 source
