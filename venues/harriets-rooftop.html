How to get in

Reserve via SevenRooms or queue. The pool deck loungers are hotel-guests-only, but “Sky Swim Lounge” tables let non-guests buy pool access. Check Instagram for weather closures before you go.

The honest take

Arguably the best bridge-and-skyline sightline in the city. The costs: $26–31 cocktails, sunset elevator waits, and weather closures that catch people out. The DJ day-parties are the move.
