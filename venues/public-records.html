Public Records — Gowanus

Status: Open — dense event calendar on Dice through fall 2026; a second, larger venue in Sunset Park is expected late 2026.

Last verified: 2026-08-25 — every fact below is sourced; anything we couldn't confirm is marked as reported or unverified.

The facts

Address233 Butler St, Brooklyn, NY 11217
NeighborhoodGowanus, Brooklyn
TypeMulti-space venue (est. 2017): hi-fi Sound Room with custom OJAS sound system, Atrium restaurant/venue, UPSTAIRS listening bar, and the seasonal open-air Nursery
MusicDeep/dub techno, house, ambient, jazz and global bookings — recent names include Leon Vynehall, Erol Alkan, upsammy, Benji B
Nights & hoursEvents roughly Wed–Sun. Sound Room club nights typically 10pm–4am; Nursery daytime parties on Sundays (seasonal through ~October). Full weekly hours not confirmed on an official page (reported)
Cover / entry costDice tickets: free to ~$42 all-in. Most Sound Room nights start around $22 first tier rising to ~$32; live shows ~$27–$37; big Nursery all-day events ~$42 (fees-included Dice prices, 2026-08-25)
Dress codeNone official (unverified); stylish-casual reported
Age policySound Room nights reported 21+; restaurant and cafe spaces all-ages (reported)
Tables & reservationsNo club tables. Atrium restaurant takes dinner reservations; UPSTAIRS is partly walk-in (reported)
Peak timesFri/Sat midnight–3am in the Sound Room; Nursery Sundays sell out in summer (reported)
Official sitepublicrecords.nyc
Instagram@publicrecordsnyc

How to get in

Everything sells on Dice — buy the early tier (saves about $10) and use Dice's waiting list for sold-out shows; it works. The door is friendly with no dress-code drama. For summer Nursery daytimes, buy the moment they're announced.

The honest take

Arguably the best-sounding room in NYC and genuinely adventurous booking, but it's the most polished of the underground venues — drinks are pricey, the crowd can skew fashion/media, and the Sound Room is small enough that floor space disappears fast. Gowanus is a dead zone for late-night transit.
