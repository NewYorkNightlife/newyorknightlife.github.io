Short answer

Public Records is a venue in Gowanus, Brooklyn. No dress code is posted. Cover: The restaurant, cafe and bar charge no entry fee. Sound Room shows and club nights are ticketed on DICE, e.g. a jazz show on Oct 24, 2026 from ~$32. Hours: Campus hours: Wed-Thu 6-10pm, Fri 6pm-4am, Sat 10am-4am, Sun 10am-9pm; closed Mon-Tue. Questions

What is the dress code at Public Records?

No dress code is posted. The official site and the reviews checked (The Infatuation, Xceed) mention no dress code.

How old do you have to be for Public Records?

We have not found a published age policy.

Is there a cover at Public Records?

The restaurant, cafe and bar charge no entry fee. Sound Room shows and club nights are ticketed on DICE, e.g. a jazz show on Oct 24, 2026 from ~$32.

What time does Public Records close?

Campus hours: Wed-Thu 6-10pm, Fri 6pm-4am, Sat 10am-4am, Sun 10am-9pm; closed Mon-Tue. Event times vary.

How do you get into Public Records?

Book Atrium and UPSTAIRS tables on Resy. Buy Sound Room and Nursery event tickets on DICE (a few events go through partner pages). Booking link is the venue's own page.
