Magic Hour Rooftop Bar & Lounge — Times Square

Status: Open — official Moxy Times Square pages current.

Last verified: 2026-08-25 — every fact below is sourced; anything we couldn't confirm is marked as reported or unverified.

How to get in

  1. Reserve a table free on the official booking widget at moxytimessquare.com — no fee to reserve, card required to hold, $30 no-show fee. This is the move for Thursday–Saturday.
  2. No reservation? Arrive before sunset and walk in to the bar — walk-in table service is first-come.
  3. Know the minimums: after 9pm Wednesday–Saturday, table minimums apply (reported, amounts vary) — confirm when booking. Cocktails run ~$18–$22 (reported).
  4. Bring physical government ID — 21+ at all times except weekend brunch (Sat–Sun 11:30am–3pm), and a foreign ID needs a passport behind it (official).
  5. Dress “fun, casual, no true athletic wear” (official); no shorts or sandals after 9pm (reported).
  6. Tables run in 90-minute blocks; cancel by noon day-of or eat the $30 fee. Groups of 15+ go through the events team.

The honest take

The views and theme-park set pieces genuinely deliver for photos, and the seasonal takeovers keep it fresh. Downsides: it's a tourist-heavy Times Square scene with resort-level drink prices, 90-minute table limits, and a long weekend door line — locals treat it as a one-time novelty, not a regular.

Getting there & getting home

Magic Hour sits on the 18th floor of the Moxy NYC Times Square at 485 Seventh Ave (at 36th St), in the middle of the best transit web in the city. 34th St–Herald Sq (B, D, F, M, N, Q, R, W) is two blocks away, Penn Station (1, 2, 3, A, C, E, plus LIRR and NJ Transit) three, and Times Sq–42nd St a short walk north. Everything runs 24/7, and on Fri/Sat the bar itself runs to 4am. Rideshare pickups in the garment district's side streets beat waiting on Seventh Ave.

FAQ

Does Magic Hour have a cover charge?

No general cover and no fee to reserve (official FAQ; card required to hold, $30 no-show fee). But table minimums apply after 9pm Wednesday–Saturday (reported, amounts vary).

Is Magic Hour 21+?

Yes, at all times except weekend brunch (Sat–Sun 11:30am–3pm). Physical government ID is required, and a foreign ID needs a passport (official).

What is the dress code at Magic Hour?

Official: “fun, casual, no true athletic wear.” No shorts or sandals after 9pm (reported).

What are Magic Hour's hours?

Mon–Wed 5pm–12am; Thu 5pm–2am; Fri 5pm–4am; Sat 11:30am–4am; Sun 11:30am–12am. Brunch Sat–Sun 11:30am–3:30pm (official, fetched 2026-08-25).

How do table reservations work at Magic Hour?

Book free via the official widget on moxytimessquare.com. Tables run 90-minute blocks; cancel by noon day-of or a $30 fee applies. Walk-in table service is first-come; groups of 15+ go via the events team.

When is the best time to go to Magic Hour?

Golden hour (~6–8pm) for the light, Fri/Sat 10pm–1am for the party, and weekend brunch in summer — all three get slammed (reported).
