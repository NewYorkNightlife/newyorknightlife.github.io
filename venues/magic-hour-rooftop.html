Magic Hour Rooftop Bar & Lounge

Dress code
Fun, casual; no athletic wear

The official FAQ says 'fun, casual, no true athletic wear'; The Rooftop Guide also lists no shorts or sandals after 9pm.

Age
21+ except weekend brunch (Sat-Sun 11:30am-3pm, all ages); physical government ID, non-US ID needs a passport
Cover
None listed; no minimum or fee to reserve ($30 no-show fee on the card)
Getting in
Walk-ins welcome at the bar; tables first-come via the door team. Reserve on OpenTable (card required, 90-minute table block, 15-minute grace period).
Hours
Mon-Wed 5pm-12am; Thu 5pm-2am; Fri 5pm-4am; Sat 11:30am-4am; Sun 11:30am-12am (brunch Sat-Sun 11:30am-3:30pm)
Drinks
Happy hour Mon-Fri 5-6:30pm at the bar: $6 Estrella, $8 house wine, $10 spritzes. Regular cocktails reported as typical-to-high Manhattan rooftop prices (no menu prices found).
