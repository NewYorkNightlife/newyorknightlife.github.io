Magic Hour Rooftop Bar & Lounge — Times Square

The facts

Address485 Seventh Ave (18th floor, Moxy NYC Times Square), New York, NY 10018
NeighborhoodTimes Square, Manhattan
Type10,000 sq ft indoor/outdoor hotel rooftop (Tao Group) — an "urban amusement park" with carousel seating, mini-golf, and rotating seasonal themes

Cover / entry costNo general cover and no fee to reserve (official FAQ; card required to hold, $30 no-show fee). Table minimums apply after 9pm Wed–Sat (reported, amounts vary). Cocktails ~$18–$22 (reported)
Dress codeOfficial: "fun, casual, no true athletic wear." No shorts/sandals after 9pm (reported)

Peak timesSunset golden hour (~6–8pm) and Fri/Sat 10pm–1am; weekend brunch slammed in summer (reported)

Instagram@magichourrooftopnyc (reported; hotel is @moxytimessquare)

How to get in

Reserve a table on the official booking widget (free, card hold) especially Thu–Sat; otherwise arrive before sunset and walk in to the bar. After 9pm Wed–Sat expect table minimums — confirm when booking. Bring physical 21+ ID; skip gym clothes.

The honest take

The views and theme-park set pieces genuinely deliver for photos, and the seasonal takeovers keep it fresh. Downsides: it's a tourist-heavy Times Square scene with resort-level drink prices, 90-minute table limits, and a long weekend door line — locals treat it as a one-time novelty, not a regular.
