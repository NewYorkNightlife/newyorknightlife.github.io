Bossa Nova Civic Club — Bushwick

Status: Open — active August 2026 events on RA and current guide listings.

Last verified: 2026-08-25 — every fact below is sourced; anything we couldn't confirm is marked as reported or unverified.

The facts

Address1271 Myrtle Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11221
NeighborhoodBushwick, Brooklyn
Type~140-capacity fog-heavy techno bar/club under the M-train overpass, open since 2012 (reopened after a 2022 fire)
MusicTechno, house, electro, experimental — underground and local-first, no EDM; live hardware sets and occasional touring guests
Nights & hoursOpen roughly 7 nights, about 7pm–4am (reported via listings; the official site publishes minimal info)
Cover / entry costFree on many weeknights and early hours; typically ~$10–$15 cash at the door, more for special lineups (reported). Mostly a door-cash operation with limited advance tickets via RA for bigger nights
Dress codeNone official; all-black/club casual reported
Age policy21+ (reported — operates as a bar)
Tables & reservationsNone. No VIP anything.
Peak times1–4am, especially Fri/Sat (reported)

Instagram@bossanovacivicclub

How to get in

Just show up with cash and a 21+ ID — the door is first-come and rarely selective, but expect a wait after 1am on weekends. Check RA day-of since many nights are free before a certain hour.

The honest take

The beating heart of cheap, no-frills Bushwick techno — $0–$15 in, decent sound for the size, zero pretense. Downsides: tiny and genuinely cramped at peak, very hot and foggy, bathroom lines, cash-only covers, and quality varies night to night since it's local-DJ-driven.
