Status: Open — official Tao Group page current. Not a late-night venue: closes by midnight at the latest and is closed Mondays.

How to get in

Walk through the pawn shop at 146 Essex to the hidden staircase. Bar/lounge areas take walk-ins; dining rooms via OpenTable/Resy (bookable 45 days out; parties of 15+ by email).

The honest take

The pawn-shop reveal and chandelier staircase still land for dates and birthdays — but go for the scene, not the food, which recent reviews call loud, touristy, and overpriced.
