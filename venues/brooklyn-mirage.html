Brooklyn Mirage — East Williamsburg

Status: CLOSED as a brand — the venue reopened as Pacha New York in June 2026. This page explains what happened.

Last verified: 2026-08-25 — every fact below is sourced; anything we couldn't confirm is marked as reported or unverified.

How to get in

You can't — the Brooklyn Mirage no longer exists. For events at the same address, see Pacha New York: buy presale at pacha-nyc.com, bring a physical 21+ ID, and expect the highest GA prices in Brooklyn.

The honest take

The Mirage as people knew it is gone. The timeline: it never reopened after December 2024; parent company Avant Gardner filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy in August 2025 with about $155M in debt; the complex sold for roughly $110M; partial demolition followed; and FIVE Holdings (owner of Ibiza's Pacha brand) relaunched the site as Pacha New York on June 20–21, 2026. Roughly $3.1M in unrefunded Mirage tickets were settled with vouchers. If you see "Brooklyn Mirage tickets" on resale sites, they're not real.
