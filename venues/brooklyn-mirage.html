Brooklyn Mirage

Status
Closed

Brooklyn Mirage never reopened after its 2025 renovation (DOB structural/safety issues; no 2025 season), and owner Avant Gardner filed Chapter 11 in Oct 2025. Pacha's parent FIVE Holdings took over in early 2026 and the site reopened as Pacha New York, a seasonal open-air club (June–Oct 2026; 'Open Air Season Closing' listed for Oct 18, 2026). A year-round indoor room, Brooklyn Pacha, opens in the Great Hall at the same address on Nov 6–7, 2026.
