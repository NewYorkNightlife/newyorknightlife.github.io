How to get in

Call ahead for a dinner reservation if you want a guaranteed seat; otherwise walk in and give your name for the bar. Go before 8pm or after 1am to avoid the crush. Look for the pink neon "Psychic" sign — that's the door.

The honest take

Still one of the definitive NYC cocktail bars, with the rare virtue of a real 4am kitchen. Downsides: it's been famous for 20+ years, so expect tourists, a crush at the bar on weekends, and hour-plus waits without a dinner reservation.
