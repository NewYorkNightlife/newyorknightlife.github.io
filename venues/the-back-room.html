The Back Room — Lower East Side

Status: Open — official site current. Unusual policy worth knowing: 25+ on Friday and Saturday nights.

Last verified: 2026-08-25 — every fact below is sourced; anything we couldn't confirm is marked as reported or unverified.

The facts

Address102 Norfolk St, New York, NY 10002 — through the “Lower East Side Toy Company” gate, down the alley, up the metal stairs
NeighborhoodLower East Side, Manhattan
TypeGenuine Prohibition-era speakeasy — cocktails in teacups, beer in paper bags
MusicDJs Thursday–Saturday 9pm–close; free Monday night jazz (music 9pm–12:30am)
Nights & hoursSun–Mon 6pm–1am; Tue–Thu 6pm–2am; Fri–Sat 6pm–3am
Cover / entry costNo cover — “Always Free” per the official site
Dress codeOfficial code: no baseball hats, no flip-flops, no tank tops (men), no real fur
Age policy21+ Sunday–Thursday; 25+ Friday & Saturday (official — and rarely reported anywhere else)
Tables & reservationsWalk-ins and reservations both accepted
Peak timesWeekend DJ nights get packed; Monday jazz is the connoisseur move
Official sitebackroomnyc.com
Instagram@thebackroomnyc

How to get in

Find the alley entrance at 102 Norfolk and walk in, or reserve on the site. Check ahead for private-event closures.

The honest take

One of only two NYC bars that actually operated during Prohibition — real history, free entry, real late hours. Weekends run young, loud, and packed; drinks are solid rather than exceptional.
