Lower East Side · cocktail bar

The Back Room

Dress code
No hats, flip-flops, men's tanks or real fur

The official site bans baseball hats, flip-flops, tank tops on men and real fur (faux fur, leather and suede are fine); Time Out repeats the same rules.

Checked Oct 18, 2026 · 6 sources · editor review pendingSources ↓
Age
21+ Sun–Thu; 25+ Fri–Sat
Cover
None ("always free")
Getting in
Walk-ins welcome. Reserve through the venue site (taken until 11:30pm, Tue–Sun). Small-group reservations have a bar minimum, large groups need bottle service Fri–Sat, and bachelor/bachelorette parties aren't allowed. Enter through the 'Lower East Side Toy Company' gate.
Hours
Sun–Mon 6pm–1am; Tue–Thu 6pm–2am; Fri–Sat 6pm–3am
Drinks
Moderate (Time Out $$ of 4); exact cocktail prices aren't published, and some reviewers call the drinks small or pricey
