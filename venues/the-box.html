The Box — Lower East Side

Status: Open (per 2026 listings). The official FAQ's stated nights (Wed–Sat) date to older site content — confirm by phone (917-280-5977) before a big night.

Last verified: 2026-08-25 — every fact below is sourced; anything we couldn't confirm is marked as reported or unverified.

The facts

Address189 Chrystie St, New York, NY 10002
NeighborhoodLower East Side, Manhattan
Type“Theatre of Varieties” — theatrical burlesque/variety nightclub with bottle-service economics
MusicVariety/burlesque stage shows roughly every 30 minutes, open-format music between
Nights & hoursWednesday–Saturday, roughly 11pm–4am; dark Sunday–Tuesday
Cover / entry costNo standard GA cover — reservations are effectively required for entry; without one it's the doorman's call. Table minimums reported around $500+, bottles ~$500–650 (third-party, fluctuates by night)
Dress codeDress to impress — formal leaning; no sportswear, sneakers, or hats (reported; the venue's own FAQ answer is “there's only one way to find out…”)
Age policy21+ (standard; not published officially)
Tables & reservationsVia theboxnyc.com/booking or 917-280-5977; ~$500+ minimums reported
Peak timesShows run late — this is a midnight-to-4am room
Official sitetheboxnyc.com
Instagram@theboxnyc

How to get in

Book a table via theboxnyc.com or phone. Walk-up entry is discretionary — the venue's own formula: “vibe multiplied by style divided by how many people you're with.” Phones and cameras prohibited inside.

The honest take

Manhattan's last true late-night theatrical spectacle — genuinely transgressive shows in a gorgeous room. Expensive, selectively doored, and phone-free; reviews split between “unforgettable” and “needed therapy after.”
