Status: Open — official calendar shows events every weekend through December 2026.

The facts

How to get in

Buy RA presale — door price is higher and popular nights like FIXED or Eli Escobar all-nighters can sell through. The door is generally easy if you're 21+ with ID. Arrive before midnight to skip the line.

The honest take

One of the most reliable mid-size dance rooms in the city — real DJ bookings, proper sound, unpretentious door. Downsides: the main room gets packed and sweaty at peak, Bad Room can feel like an afterthought on off nights, and the edge-of-Greenpoint location is annoying to reach late.
