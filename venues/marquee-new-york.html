How to get in

Tickets via taogroup.com. A “Table Share Experience” product includes admission at a shared table. Expect a line and a selective door — dress up and arrive before peak.

The honest take

The archetypal Manhattan mega-club, freshly renovated — expanded dance floor, upgraded LEDs, A-list DJs, and a dressed-up bottle-service crowd. Long lines, selective door, expensive drinks: it's a splurge night, not a dive.
