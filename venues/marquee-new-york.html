Marquee New York is a club in Chelsea, Manhattan. Dress code: None published; upscale expected. Cover: Ticketed per night; a Marquee Wednesdays listing showed tickets from $82 (Oct 2026); resale averages ~$76. Hours: Wed, Fri, Sat 11pm-4am. Facts checked 2026-10-18.
