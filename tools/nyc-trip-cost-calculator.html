How the estimate works

Every line is a range pulled from prices we verify, not a national travel average. Lodging multiplies your tier's nightly rate by your nights; food and nightlife apply a verified per-day band for each style; transit assumes about four subway taps a day at $3.00 and applies the OMNY fare cap — once you've spent $35 in a rolling 7-day window, the rest of that week's rides are free, automatically. That cap is why the subway line flattens at $35 from day three: on any trip up to a week, transit simply cannot cost more than $35 if you tap the same card every ride.

Treat the low end of the result as the disciplined version of your trip — presale tickets, happy-hour timing, January hotel rates — and the high end as the September-Saturday version with a third round and a 3am car. Both are real; which one you get is mostly decided before you leave the hotel.

Where trips blow the budget

  • The airport cab, twice. AirTrain + subway from JFK is about $11.65; a cab with tolls and tip is $70–$90+. That one choice, made both ways, swings the trip by roughly $120–$150.
  • Booking the wrong month. The same hostel dorm averages ~$52 in January and $175+ in September. Hotels follow the same curve. If your dates are flexible, this is the single biggest lever on the whole estimate.
  • Late-night cars instead of the 24-hour subway. A $3 ride home at 3am versus a $35–$60 surge-priced car, several nights running. Which lines are reliable at that hour is covered in our subway for nightlife guide.
  • Paying the door instead of presale. Ticketed rooms tier their pricing — buying the week before can halve your entry. Run any specific club through the cover charge calculator.
  • Sleeping far from where you go out. A hotel 15 minutes from your nightlife zone saves both money and momentum — our where to stay for nightlife guide maps the trade-offs.

FAQ

How much does a 3-night NYC trip cost per person in 2026?

By the verified bands: roughly $330–$600 all-in as a backpacker, $960–$1,650 mid-range, and $2,200+ in the comfort tier — before flights. Run your own dials above for the itemized version.

What's the cheapest way from JFK to Manhattan?

AirTrain + subway, about $11.65 total, versus $70–$90+ for a cab with the flat fare, tolls, and tip. Budget 50–75 minutes for the train to most of Manhattan.

How does the $35 OMNY weekly cap work?

Tap the same contactless card or phone for every $3.00 ride; once you've spent $35 in a rolling 7-day window, further subway and local bus rides are free. Nothing to buy in advance — it just happens. MetroCards are being retired; don't buy one.

How much should I budget per night for nightlife?

$25–$50 for happy hours and no-cover dives, $60–$120 for a cocktail bar plus a show or club, $150–$300+ once big-room covers and late cars enter the night. Covers alone run $0–$15 at dives and $20–$70+ at clubs — see the venue fact pages for specific doors.
