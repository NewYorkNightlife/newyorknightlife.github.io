NYC Nightlife Tools

Fourteen small, free tools, each built to answer one planning question fast. No signups — pick the question you're stuck on and open the tool that solves it.

Build a plan

  • NYC Night Planner — the flagship: turns vibe, budget, and location into a sequenced night with backups.
  • 3-Day Itinerary Builder — for visitors stacking multiple nights without repeating themselves.
  • Group Night Out Planner — herding six people with different budgets into one plan.
  • Birthday Night Planner — birthday-specific: where groups get in easily and what to book ahead.
  • Night Out Spinner — can't decide? Let it decide for you.

Money questions

  • Budget Planner — realistic per-person math for covers, drinks, and rides.
  • Cover Charge Calculator — what entry actually costs across venue types.
  • Trip Cost Calculator — full-visit math for out-of-towners.

Will I get in?

  • Dress Code Checker — check your outfit against typical door standards before you leave.
  • Venue Compare — put venues side by side on cover, dress, age, and vibe, using our verified venue facts.

Where should I go?

  • Nightlife Map — the geography of NYC nightlife at a glance.
  • Neighborhood Quiz — answers "which scene fits me?" in under a minute.
  • Hotel Neighborhood Matcher — pick where to stay based on the nights you want, paired with our neighborhood guides.
  • Subway Safe Route — late-night route thinking, alongside the subway-for-nightlife guide.

Which tool first?

If you only use one, use the Night Planner — it folds budget, vibe, and sequencing into one pass. If your night hinges on a strict door (Marquee, Nebula, PHD), check Venue Compare and the venue's own fact page first, because no plan survives being turned away at 12:30am. And if the group can't agree, the group vibe decider in our games section settles it faster than a group chat. For questions mid-planning, the gold bubble bottom-right is Nyla, our concierge — she answers from the same verified data these tools use.

How these tools stay honest

Where a tool touches real venue facts — covers, dress codes, ages — it draws on the same dataset as our venue pages, which is re-verified on a schedule and dated (see the editorial policy). Where a tool deals in estimates, like budget math, it says so on the page. None of these tools take payments, and none require an account. They're planning aids, not booking engines: when it's time to actually buy a ticket or reserve a table, we send you to the venue's own channels, which is both safer for you and the only way to be sure prices are current.
