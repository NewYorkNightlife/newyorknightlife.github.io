How the Builder Thinks

      
The builder follows the same three rules that make the editorial itinerary work. First, one neighborhood cluster per night — every plan it produces keeps each night walkable or one short train hop, because cross-borough zigzags are where NYC nights die. Second, an energy arc: nights are sequenced from lower to higher intensity so you're still standing for the finale, and the biggest night is automatically placed on a Friday or Saturday when your dates include one. Third, every selected interest gets covered at least once across the three nights, so a group that checks rooftops, speakeasies, and clubs gets all three rather than three variations of the same bar crawl.

      
The venue pool is not generic. It is drawn from our verified venue pages: rooftops like 230 Fifth and Westlight, speakeasies like Attaboy and PDT, and clubs from House of Yes in Bushwick to Nebula in Midtown. Each night card lists realistic per-person costs — drinks, covers, and late food, not hotel or dinner splurges.

      
Day-of-week matters more than most itineraries admit. A Bushwick warehouse on a Tuesday is a different room than the same venue on a Saturday, and Friday-Saturday speakeasy waits can hit 60–90 minutes. The builder reads your actual nights and attaches warnings where your plan collides with those patterns — and tells you when going a day earlier fixes it.

FAQ

      
How many venues should I plan per night?

      
Two to three stops: one anchor (rooftop, club, or headline bar) plus one or two supporting stops in the same cluster. More than that and you spend the night in transit instead of in venues.

      
Which night should be the big club night?

      
Friday or Saturday if your dates include one — that's when club calendars are fullest and floors fill after midnight. The builder moves your highest-energy night onto a weekend night automatically. Midweek clubbing works, but check event calendars first.

      
Do I need reservations or advance tickets?

      
Weekend club nights: buy advance tickets 1–2 weeks out. PDT and Death & Co release reservations online and prime slots go fast; Attaboy and The Back Room are walk-in. Book night-one dinner about two weeks ahead for weekend dates.

      
How much will three nights cost?

      
Roughly $330–$580 per person for nightlife alone: rooftop and speakeasy cocktails $18–$25, standard bars $14–$18, dive beers $7–$9, club covers $0–$40. Last verified: 2026-08-26.
