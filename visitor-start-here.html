Start Here: How to Use NY Nightlife

This site does one job: help you decide your NYC night in 5 minutes — with real prices, real door policies, and dates on every fact. Here's the fastest path for your situation.

"What should I do tonight?"

Go to Tonight — a live feed of real, ticketable events refreshed daily, with weather and free options. Planning ahead for the weekend? This Weekend is refreshed every Thursday with actual events and their actual prices.

"Will I get in, and what will it cost?"

The Venue Facts pages are the core of this site: covers, dress codes, age policies, table minimums, and how to actually get in — every fact sourced and stamped with a last-verified date. Then price the whole night with the Budget Planner or compare two options head-to-head with Venue Compare.

"Where should we go?"

Pick the neighborhood first — it decides your night more than any single venue. Browse all neighborhood guides: Lower East Side for bar density, Bushwick/Ridgewood for real dance floors, Midtown rooftops for views, West Village for cocktails.

"I'm visiting NYC"

Start with where to stay for nightlife, read the tourist scams guide before you land, and check late-night safety by borough. The Guides hub has the rest.

Why trust this site?

Because everything is dated. A cover charge from 2023 is worse than useless — so every venue fact carries a "Last verified" stamp and a source, and we publish real downsides, not just hype. Read the full methodology. Spot something stale? Tell us — we re-verify.
