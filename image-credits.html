Image credits

Photographs on this site are ones we have a license to store, from Wikimedia Commons and Flickr under the Creative Commons licenses linked below. Each one is credited to its author, resized and cropped by us. Where a photo shows the building or the street outside rather than the room, the note says so. Ticketmaster event images, when a listing has one, are loaded from Ticketmaster next to that event and are not saved here.

Map data

Borough outlines on the homepage night map are simplified from the Department of City Planning borough boundaries on NYC Open Data. The city publishes that file as public data. A room is placed only when the house number, street, borough, and ZIP on its page match an address point from NYC Planning Labs GeoSearch, which reads the Department of City Planning PAD. Rooms that do not match are left off the map.
