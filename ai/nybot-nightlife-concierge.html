Nyla: NYC Nightlife Concierge — How She Works

Nyla (formerly "NYBot") is the concierge chat you'll find in the bottom-right corner of every page on NY Nightlife. This page explains what she knows, how she decides what to tell you, and where her limits are — because an honest tool should show its wiring.

What she knows

Two data sources, both published on this site and nothing else:

  • The venue facts file — the same dataset behind our 30 venue fact pages. For each venue it holds the cover charge, dress code, age policy, hours, table/bottle policy, peak times, a step-by-step "how to get in," and an honest verdict. Every entry carries a "last verified" date so you can judge freshness yourself.
  • The tonight feed — a daily-updated list of what's on, powering the Tonight page.

Questions she's good at

  • "What's the cover at 230 Fifth?" — exact published policy, quoted from the dated data.
  • "Which rooftops are free?" — she compares cover fields across all rooftop venues.
  • "Is Marquee strict at the door?" — dress and door policy, flagged as official or reported.
  • "What's happening tonight?" — straight from the daily feed.
  • "Cheap night out?" — venues with low or no cover, plus the under-$50 ranking.
  • "Where should I start for a weekend plan?" — she'll route you to the weekend hub and the night planner.

How she avoids making things up

Nyla is deliberately rules-based rather than free-wheeling: she matches your question against the site's data and quotes what's there. When a fact is "reported" rather than officially published by a venue, our data labels it that way and Nyla passes the label through. When she has no data — a venue we don't cover, a date beyond the feed — she says so instead of improvising. That mirrors the site-wide sourcing rules in our editorial policy.

Privacy

Chats happen in your browser. Nyla doesn't ask for personal details and doesn't need any to work. See the privacy policy for the site-wide picture.

Try her now

Click the gold bubble at the bottom right of this page and ask about any of the 30 venues — covers, dress, age, hours — or just say "tonight." If something she says looks out of date, the venue page it came from shows the verification date, and our contact page is the fastest way to flag it.
