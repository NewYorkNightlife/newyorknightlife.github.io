Harriet's Rooftop. Official 1 Hotels page live with current hours and events (an event dated Fri 23 Oct); open weather permitting, updates on Instagram. Separate indoor Harriet's Lounge one floor below.

Le Bain. Official site lists events through Halloween 2026 (Dirty Mag Halloween Oct 29, Susanne Bartsch Boom! Oct 30, Halloween on the 18th Floor Oct 31). Penthouse club plus The Rooftop bar on the top floor of The Standard, High Line.

Magic Hour Rooftop Bar & Lounge. Official Moxy page is current: 'Dark Magic Hour' Halloween takeover runs through Oct 31, 2026. Operated by Tao Group; a reviewer notes it is run separately from the hotel (no room charges, no direct access from hotel elevators).

Mr. Purple. Official site is current, with a fall menu, 2026 hours, and live SevenRooms and SpeakeasyGo booking links. Hours have changed since August: it now opens at 4pm on weekdays, not 1pm.

Ophelia Lounge. The official site is current: autumn installation, Halloween Weekend, Omacocktail tasting, a $60 prix-fixe dinner and live OpenTable booking. Sunday now opens at 5pm, not noon. The site's own meta description still shows the old Sunday 12pm opening, but the posted hours and The Rooftop Guide both say 5pm.

PHD Rooftop Lounge. Appears open. The Tao Group official page still lists regular Wed-Sat hours and SevenRooms VIP booking. The Rooftop Guide updated its listing Apr 27, 2026, and a New Year's Eve 2026 ticket page is live. The Tao page also shows a generic 'Closed for the Season' pop-up that doesn't name a venue; ambiguous, so the status rests on the hours listing. The hotel is now branded 'Dream Downtown, by Hyatt'.

Somewhere Nowhere. Official site lists events for Oct 18–Nov 7, 2026. Hours changed since August: Thursday is now listed as closed (was 12pm–11pm). Instagram handle not confirmed from an official page.

Westlight. Official site is current. Changes since August: Friday now closes at 1am (was 2am), and reservations now go through Tablz (app.tablz.com/westlight), not OpenTable.

Questions

Which rooftop bars did you check?

230 Fifth, Harriet's Rooftop, Le Bain, Magic Hour, Mr. Purple, Ophelia Lounge, PHD Rooftop Lounge, Somewhere Nowhere, and Westlight. Le Bain is filed as a club with a rooftop tag. The others are rooftop bars. This page does not rank them.

Is there a cover?

It is not one number. 230 Fifth publishes $15 on Friday and $20 on Saturday from 8pm, checked 2026-10-18. Le Bain lists no cover for the standing room and a minimum on tables. PHD's cover is unconfirmed. The other pages say none listed, a table minimum, or that a ticket price is not published. Read the venue page.

What if the weather is bad?

Harriet's hours say the rooftop opens weather permitting. We do not have a weather rule for the others. 230 Fifth's hours are unconfirmed and say Friday has no published closing time.

Are they 21+?

Not all of them, and not all night. 230 Fifth is 21+ Friday and Saturday after 7pm. Westlight is all ages until 6pm and 21+ after. Magic Hour is 21+ except weekend brunch. Several pages publish no age. The age line on the venue page is the one we checked.
