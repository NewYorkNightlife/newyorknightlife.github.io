Short answer

New Year's Eve 2026 is Thursday, December 31. New York City dates the Times Square ball drop that night. The Alliance FAQ says the ball goes up around 6:00pm EST and starts a 60-second descent at 11:59pm EST, and that the outdoor viewing areas open at 3pm with no ticket. On-premises alcohol sales still stop at 4am. This page does not publish a cruise or party price. Official pages checked 2026-11-19.

Times Square ball drop

New York City's press-card page titles the event the 2027 New Year's Eve ball drop and dates it Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026. That page is about press credentials, not about public viewing.

The Times Square Alliance FAQ, checked 2026-11-19, opens with New Year's Eve 2027. It says the ball descends from a flagpole at the top of One Times Square, best seen along Broadway from 43rd Street to 50th Street and along Seventh Avenue as far north as 59th Street. The celebration begins shortly before 6:00pm EST. At about 6:00pm EST the ball is raised. At 11:59pm EST it makes a 60-second descent. Viewing areas open at 3pm on a first-come, first-served basis. The FAQ says no ticket is needed and that no public viewing space is for sale. Access points it lists: 45th, 49th, 52nd, and 56th Streets, from 6th Avenue and from 8th Avenue.

The same FAQ says all bags are searched. It says backpacks, large bags, umbrellas, lawn chairs, alcohol, drones, and weapons are not permitted, and that there are no portable restrooms in Times Square during the celebration. The designated viewing area for people with disabilities is at 44th Street and Broadway, reached from the southwest corner of 44th Street and 6th Avenue.

One line on that FAQ does not match the heading. It says to join the webcast at 6pm on December 31 for the NYE 2026 celebration. The City page above dates the drop December 31, 2026. Read the Alliance page again before you go. This guide does not copy the performer list on the FAQ. The FAQ does not date that list to December 31, 2026.

The FAQ also says Notify NYC takes the keyword NEWYEARSEVE at 692692 for weather and street alerts. It says message and data rates may apply.

Cruises

Circle Line's New Year's Eve page, checked 2026-11-19, describes two sailings for ringing in 2027. Both board at Pier 83, Midtown, 83 North River Piers, New York, NY 10036, at 9:00pm, and run 10:00pm to 1:00am. The dinner cruise is all ages and lists a buffet, an open bar, a dessert display, a live DJ, a champagne toast, and fireworks views. The party cruise is 21+ and lists a live DJ, an open standard bar, passed appetizers, a champagne toast at midnight, and fireworks views near the Statue of Liberty. That page does not print a calendar date or a ticket price, so this guide does not either. Dinner and sunset cruises has the other piers we have checked, and says a sunset cruise on another night is not an NYE ticket.

Parties and bars

The Alliance FAQ says some restaurants and hotels in Times Square hold private parties that need a ticket bought ahead. It warns that multi-venue, pay-one-price, all-access passes have advertised movement through Times Square that police checkpoints have not allowed. Last call is still 4am

The closing-time guide cites NY ABC Law §106(5): no on-premises alcohol sales from 4am to 8am, or from 4am to 10am on Sunday, and no drinking on the premises more than half an hour after 4am. It says the State Liquor Authority's county closing hours list 4am for all five boroughs. That guide checked the law text on 2026-10-23. Its citation of section 106(5) does not describe a New Year's Eve exception. A venue can close earlier. This weekend is the Friday-to-Sunday feed, which is a different night from December 31.

Questions

When is New Year's Eve 2026?

Thursday, December 31, 2026. New York City's Mayor's Office of Media and Entertainment dates the 2027 Times Square ball drop that day. The Times Square Alliance FAQ, checked the same day as this page, calls the season New Year's Eve 2027.

What time is the ball drop?

The Times Square Alliance FAQ says the celebration begins shortly before 6:00pm EST, the ball is raised at about 6:00pm EST, and at 11:59pm EST it starts a 60-second descent from the flagpole at One Times Square. Viewing areas open at 3pm, first come, first served, with no ticket. The same FAQ also tells people to watch a webcast at 6pm on December 31 and calls that stream the NYE 2026 celebration.

What time do NYC bars close on New Year's Eve?

4am, according to the closing-time guide on this site. That guide cites New York ABC Law section 106(5): no on-premises alcohol sales from 4am to 8am, and no drinking on the premises more than half an hour after 4am. The guide's citation does not describe a New Year's Eve exception. December 31, 2026 is a Thursday, so the Sunday-morning rule in that section is not the one in effect that night. Many venues close earlier. Weekly hours on a venue page are not an NYE schedule.

How much is an NYE cruise?

Circle Line's New Year's Eve page does not print a price, and this guide does not invent one. The page describes two Pier 83 sailings for ringing in 2027 and does not print a calendar date. Read the operator's listing. Viator links below are a separate search.
