Under 21 in NYC: which bars and clubs let you in

In New York you must be 21 to buy or drink alcohol. Whether you can walk in under 21 is up to each venue. Of the 29 venues we've checked, 7 let some under-21s in, at some times, with conditions.

  • The law: no one may sell or give alcohol to anyone under 21 (NY ABC Law §65), and under-21s may not possess it intending to drink (§65-c).
  • Entry age is each venue's own policy, and it often changes by night or event. Read the conditions, then check the event listing.
  • Bring a physical government photo ID. 3 of the venues we have checked say a non-US ID must be a passport; see each venue page.
