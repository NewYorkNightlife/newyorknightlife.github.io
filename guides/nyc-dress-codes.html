The roundup

NYC bar & club dress codes

What 29 New York bars and clubs say about dress code, age and cover, and what sources report when the venue says nothing. Checked Oct 18, 2026.

The short version

  • Most cocktail bars publish no dress code. "Smart casual" is the safe default.
  • Clubs and rooftops are where sneakers, athletic wear and hats get turned away.
  • Almost every venue here is 21+ at night. Bring a physical ID.
