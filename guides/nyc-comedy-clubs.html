Comedy Cellar

The homepage is the price list. On 2026-11-16 it said every show has a two-item minimum per person, and that the item can be food, a drink, or a bottle of water. Friday and Saturday shows were $25. Thursday and Sunday shows were $18. Monday to Wednesday shows were $15, except New Joke Night at $10 and Hot Soup Comedy at $20. Holidays and special events were $25.

The contact page gives two entrances. 117 MacDougal Street is the Comedy Cellar and the Olive Tree Cafe. 130 West 3rd Street is the Village Underground and Fat Black Pussycat, which has a bar and a lounge. The phone on that page is 212-254-3480. It tells ticket holders to bring the QR code from the reservation email to the entrance named in that email.

Gotham Comedy Club

Gotham's site lists the club at 208 West 23rd Street, between 7th and 8th Avenues, New York, NY 10011, phone 212-367-9000. The same pages describe pub food, a liquor selection that includes 30 kinds of vodka, and a wine list. The schedule names the main room and The Vintage Lounge, including a Kids 'N Comedy show. It does not publish a cover or a dress code on the pages we opened, so those are omitted.

The Stand

The Stand's site lists two rooms: 116 East 16th Street, New York, NY 10003 (212-677-2600) and The Stand West at 407 West 15th Street, New York, NY 10011. Under hours it lists Monday to Thursday 12pm–12am, Friday 12pm–2am, Saturday 11am–2am, and Sunday 11am–12am. The cocktails line says they do not need a two-drink minimum. The contact page points private-party questions to a private parties page. We did not copy that page's packages.

New York Comedy Club

New York Comedy Club's site lists three rooms and three phones: Midtown at 241 East 24th Street, New York, NY 10010, (212) 696-5233; East Village at 85 East 4th Street, New York, NY 10003, (212) 994-9828; Upper West Side at 236 West 78th Street, New York, NY 10024, (973) 222-7422. The homepage is a changing lineup, so this guide does not repeat the bill.

Questions

Which comedy clubs did you verify?

Comedy Cellar (117 MacDougal Street, plus Village Underground and Fat Black Pussycat at 130 West 3rd Street), Gotham Comedy Club at 208 West 23rd Street, The Stand at 116 East 16th Street and The Stand West at 407 West 15th Street, and New York Comedy Club at 241 East 24th Street, 85 East 4th Street, and 236 West 78th Street. This page does not rank them.

What does Comedy Cellar charge?

On 2026-11-16 the Comedy Cellar homepage listed a two-item minimum per person (food, a drink, or a bottle of water). Friday and Saturday shows were $25, Thursday and Sunday shows $18, and Monday to Wednesday shows $15, with New Joke Night at $10 and Hot Soup Comedy at $20. Holidays and special events were $25. Check the homepage before you go.

Does The Stand have a drink minimum?

The Stand's homepage says its cocktails do not come with a two-drink minimum. That is the club's line, not a price. The site lists hours of Monday to Thursday 12pm to 12am, Friday 12pm to 2am, Saturday 11am to 2am, and Sunday 11am to 12am.

Where are tonight's comedy shows?

The list on this page is whatever the events feed marked as comedy, or titled as comedy, and that has not already ended. If it is empty, the feed did not include one. Lineups on the club sites change and are not copied here.
