NYC boat party

A ticketed night on the harbor, not a private yacht quote. These are the sailings the operators describe, checked 2026-11-16.

Short answer

For an NYC boat party, Circle Line's Sip 'N Groove is the 18+ DJ cruise from Pier 83. Harbor Lights (Pier 83, 2 hours) and Statue at Sunset (Pier 16, about 90 minutes) are sightseeing cruises. City Cruises' Signature Dinner Cruise is 2.5 hours from Pier 61 with a dinner buffet, a live DJ, and a casual dress code. We are not publishing a ticket price. Checked 2026-11-16.

The party cruise

Circle Line's own comparison of its evening cruises calls Sip 'N Groove a two-hour 18+ party cruise from Pier 83 with a live DJ and a full bar. The same page says it fits birthdays, bachelorette weekends, and groups of friends. It does not list a clock time or a dress code. Alcohol service is still 21+ in New York. Under 21 in NYC cites the statute. Boarding age is the 18+ rule printed for this sailing.

Sunset and night sightseeing

Harbor Lights is a two-hour narrated cruise from Pier 83 with open-air decks, a full bar, and concessions. Circle Line's Harbor Lights page says groups of 15 or more can get special pricing, and that cocktails, beer, wine, soft drinks, and catered food can be added. It does not print that price.

On the schedule page, Harbor Lights is 7pm in the off-peak, shoulder, and peak seasons, and 5pm in the winter season dated January 5 to March 5. The evening-cruise comparison instead says 7pm most of the year and 6pm from January through March. Both pages say the calendar is what to book against, and the schedule page says times can change and that holiday sailings replace the regular cruise.

Statue at Sunset is about 90 minutes from Pier 16 at the South Street Seaport, with music and drinks and no narration. Its departure is the one Circle Line moves to stay near sunset. The schedule lists it as limited in winter, about 4:00–5:30pm off-peak, about 6:00–7:00pm in the shoulder, and about 7:30pm at peak.

The same comparison says Harbor Lights and Statue at Sunset can work for families who are fine with an evening departure. Sip 'N Groove is 18 and older. None of those three write a dress code.

A Holiday Harbor Lights cruise is listed on the Harbor Lights page for November 27, 2026 through January 3, 2027. That is a separate sailing from the regular Harbor Lights cruise.

Public sunset tickets we priced on October 18, 2026, and the piers we had already written up, are on NYC dinner and sunset cruises and Bachelorette Night for 10. A bachelor group using the same boats is on bachelor party ideas.

Dinner cruise

City Cruises' Signature Dinner Cruise page says the sailing is 2.5 hours from Pier 61, Chelsea Piers, West 23rd Street and 12th Avenue, New York, NY. Boarding is 30 minutes before the start. The page lists a dinner buffet, a live DJ, alcoholic drinks for sale, a casual dress code, a table for the party, and an open-air rooftop lounge. Children are welcome and infants under three cruise free. The page shows a starting price that changes with the date, so this guide does not print a number.

Questions

What counts as an NYC boat party?

Circle Line describes Sip 'N Groove as an 18+ live-DJ party cruise from Pier 83, built for groups, birthdays, and a night out. Harbor Lights and Statue at Sunset are sightseeing cruises. City Cruises' Signature Dinner Cruise is a 2.5-hour dinner with a live DJ from Pier 61.

Where do the sunset cruises leave from?

Circle Line's Harbor Lights leaves from Pier 83, 83 North River Piers, New York, NY 10036. Statue at Sunset leaves from Pier 16, 89 South Street, New York, NY 10038. City Cruises' Signature Dinner Cruise boards at Pier 61, Chelsea Piers, West 23rd Street and 12th Avenue.

What time is sunset on the boat?

Circle Line's schedule lists Harbor Lights at 7pm in off-peak, shoulder, and peak seasons, and at 5pm in the winter season it dates January 5 to March 5. Its comparison page instead says 6pm from January through March. Statue at Sunset is the sailing timed near sunset, and that departure moves with the season. Check the operator's calendar for the date you want.

Can a group book together?

Circle Line's Harbor Lights page says groups of 15 or more can ask for special pricing, and that drinks and catered food can be added. It does not publish the group rate. City Cruises says the dinner cruise holds a table for the party size and that children are welcome, with infants under three cruising free.
