Best Late-Night Food Near NYC Clubs: 8 Spots Ranked (2026)

This is the ranked answer to the only question that matters at 3am: what's actually open near the club you just left, and is it worth the walk? Every pick below is matched to a nightlife zone — Lower East Side, Meatpacking, Williamsburg/Bushwick, Koreatown, Hell's Kitchen — with hours we've verified against at least the venue's own listings, plus what to order.

Ranking logic: hours reliability first (a great spot that's closed is worth nothing), then food quality, then proximity to actual clubs. For the borough-by-borough map version of this topic, see our late-night food map guide — this page is the "best spot near your club" shortlist. 1. Kellogg's Diner — Williamsburg (24 hours, $$)

The zone: Everything in Williamsburg, and one L stop from Bushwick's warehouses.

Why it ranks #1: The revived 1926 corner diner at Metropolitan and Union reopened in 2024 and runs genuinely 24 hours, with a real kitchen turning out diner classics with a Tex-Mex streak — and late-night it fully embraces the post-club crowd, dance music and all. A true sit-down at 4am, walkable from most of north Brooklyn's venues, is the rarest thing on this list.

Order: The burger, chilaquiles, or a full breakfast. Downside: weekend post-close rush means waits around 4–5am, and it's pricier than old-Kellogg's memories suggest.

2. Katz's Delicatessen — Lower East Side (24h Friday night through Sunday, $$$)

The zone: Every LES club and bar — it's on Houston Street, minutes from all of them.

Why it ranks: The 1888 institution's official hours run continuously from Friday 8am through Sunday 11pm — meaning Friday and Saturday club nights are fully covered, ticket system, hand-carved pastrami and all. There is no more New York way to end a night.

Order: Pastrami on rye, half-sour pickles. Downside: that sandwich runs roughly $28–$30, and midweek the doors close in the late evening — this is a weekend 3am move, not a Tuesday one.

3. BCD Tofu House — Koreatown (Fri–Sat to ~5am, $$)

The zone: K-town's 32nd Street karaoke-and-lounge strip and the Midtown clubs a short walk away.

Why it ranks: Open until about 5am on Friday and Saturday (1am other nights) per its own listings, BCD serves bubbling soondubu jjigae — soft tofu stew — with banchan and a whole fried fish option. It's the single most restorative meal you can eat between a club and your bed, and K-town at 4am is an experience in itself.

Order: Soondubu combo with galbi. Downside: post-2am weekend lines are real, and it's a full sit-down commitment, not a grab-and-go.

4. Empanada Mama — Hell's Kitchen (24/7, $)

The zone: Hell's Kitchen bars and the west Midtown mega-clubs.

Why it ranks: Open 24 hours every day on 9th Avenue, with 40-plus empanada varieties for a few dollars each — fast, cheap, reliable, and exactly where the Hell's Kitchen night ends. It's the best always-open option on the West Side.

Order: The "Viagra" (seafood) empanada, plus a classic beef corn empanada. Downside: the room is small and the weekend 3am scene is chaotic; expect a wait for a table (takeout moves faster).

5. The Donut Pub — Meatpacking/Chelsea border (24 hours, $)

The zone: Meatpacking and Chelsea clubs — it's at 14th Street and 7th Avenue, a 10-minute walk from the Gansevoort strip.

Why it ranks: A 1964 counter-stool classic that went 24/7 and stayed that way — fresh donuts, crullers, and coffee all night. Meatpacking proper has surprisingly little true late-night food, so the honest answer is a short walk east to this counter.

Order: A honey-dipped cruller and drip coffee. Downside: it's donuts, not dinner — if you need salt and protein, keep walking or order two.

6. Scarr's Pizza — Lower East Side (Fri–Sat to 2am, $)

The zone: LES and Dimes Square; Orchard Street sits in the middle of the club crawl.

Why it ranks: The best slice you can get at club-exit hours, made with house-milled flour, open until 2am Fridays and Saturdays per its own site. If your night ends by 2, this beats every dollar slice within a mile — and the retro room is half the fun.

Order: A classic cheese slice, or the Hotboi if it's on. Downside: 2am is early by LES standards, and the line can outlast your patience on weekends.

7. Ray's Candy Store — East Village/LES border (~open to 4am, $)

The zone: Avenue A, between East Village bars and the LES clubs.

Why it ranks: The 1974 hole-in-the-wall counter serves egg creams, beignets, fried Oreos, and fries deep into the night — listings describe it as effectively round-the-clock with a short overnight break around 4am. It's a beloved institution and the cheapest possible dose of old New York at 3am.

Order: Beignets and a chocolate egg cream. Downside: cash-friendly counter chaos, nowhere to sit, and exact overnight hours vary — typically fine until ~4am, but confirm if it's mission-critical.

8. Artichoke Basille's Pizza — Bushwick (weekends to ~4am — confirm, $)

The zone: Steps from the Jefferson L — the exit route for Bushwick warehouse nights, House of Yes, and Elsewhere.

Why it ranks: The famously heavy artichoke-cream slice, available when Bushwick lets out — local guides list the Bushwick location running to around 4am on weekends, which matches the neighborhood's clubbing clock. The nearby taco trucks are the wildcard alternative, but truck hours shift seasonally.

Order: Half artichoke, half vodka slice. Downside: it's extremely rich after a long night, and hours flex — typically ~4am weekends, but confirm before you stake your ride home on it.

How to plan the food stop

Pick your food stop when you pick your club, not at 3:45am: the 24-hour anchors (Kellogg's, Empanada Mama, Donut Pub) never fail you, while the 2–4am closers (Scarr's, Ray's, Artichoke) need timing. FAQ

What food is open at 4am in NYC?

True 24-hour options: Kellogg's Diner (Williamsburg), Empanada Mama (Hell's Kitchen), The Donut Pub (14th St). BCD Tofu House runs to ~5am Friday–Saturday, and Katz's is open around the clock on weekend nights. Halal carts and dollar slices fill the gaps everywhere else.

Is Katz's open all night?

On weekends, effectively yes — official hours run continuously from Friday 8am to Sunday 11pm. Midweek it closes in the late evening, so don't build a Tuesday 3am plan around it.

Where do you eat after clubbing in Bushwick?

Artichoke Basille's by the Jefferson L (typically to ~4am weekends — confirm), the taco trucks near the Jefferson and Morgan stops (hours shift seasonally), or ride one L stop to 24-hour Kellogg's Diner.

How much should the late-night meal cost?

Slice or empanada run: $5–$15. Diner or tofu-stew sit-down: $20–$35. Katz's pastrami: roughly $28–$30 for the sandwich alone — worth it about once.
