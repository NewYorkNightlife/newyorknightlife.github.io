How we check

The short version: we'd rather say "not found" than guess.

What counts as checked

  • Confirmed: stated by the venue itself, or two independent sources agree.
  • 1 source: one decent source says so. Treat it as likely.
  • Unconfirmed: sources disagree or are weak.
  • Not found yet: we couldn't find it. We leave it blank instead of inventing it.

Dates

Every page shows when it was checked. The date changes only when we re-check, never automatically.

Who reviews

AI tools help gather and draft facts. "Reviewed by Dan" appears only on pages Dan has approved; others say "editor review pending".

Door reports

If you went and something was different, email us. We never hide negative reports, only spam.

Prices

Prices come from the listing or venue on the date shown, include fees where the seller shows them, and change often. Confirm before you pay.
