Rooftop success is timing + reservation strategy + dress calibration. Here’s the no-BS playbook.

Best by Intent

  • Date-night premium: Arrive 6:30–7:30pm, pre-book if possible.
  • Group flex: Midtown rooftops before 10pm have highest success.
  • Budget skyline: Happy-hour windows in Lower Manhattan.

Door Strategy

  1. Never show up with a big group without a plan.
  2. Keep footwear and outerwear polished.
  3. Lock your first rooftop early, then pivot nearby.
