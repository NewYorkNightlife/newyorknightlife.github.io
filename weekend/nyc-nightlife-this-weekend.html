NYC Nightlife This Weekend: Aug 28–30, 2026

Real events, real prices, sourced from official listings on 2026-08-25. Every pick links out to the actual ticket page — weekend nightlife NYC without the guesswork. Prices are the listed tiers at time of writing; they rise as tiers sell.

Weekend in one line: Friday belongs to MoMA PS1's final Warm Up of the season; Saturday is a Knockdown Center day-to-night monster; Sunday is for the outdoor faithful at Nowadays and Public Records.

Friday, Aug 28

  • Warm Up: TDJ, De Schuurman, keiyaA — MoMA PS1, Long Island City. 4–10pm. $25 advance / $35 door, includes gallery admission. The last Friday of the museum's 28th courtyard season. Tickets (RA)
  • Night & Day: Eli Escobar, The Carry Nation, Dee Diggs — Signal, East Williamsburg. 10pm into Saturday afternoon — a 14-hour marathon of NYC disco/house selectors. RSVP discount at the door before 11pm. Details (RA)
  • SLAYYYLAND — House of Yes, Bushwick. $17 flat — the cheapest door in the building all weekend. Tickets (Dice)
  • Vigilante rooftop: Per Hammar — Roof Terrace BK, East Williamsburg. 10pm–4am. $10–$30; free before 11pm with RSVP. Open-air minimal/house. RSVP (RA)

Saturday, Aug 29

  • Dust Til Dawn: Chaos In The CBD, Joe Claussell, Floorplan — Knockdown Center, Maspeth. Day into night. From $33. The most stacked house/techno bill of the weekend. Tickets (Dice) · Venue facts
  • BASEMENT: Jason Kendig b2b James Axon, Kilopatrah Jones — Basement (inside Knockdown Center). 10:30pm–7am, 21+, strict no-phones. Tickets via RA. Details (RA)
  • Nonstop: Voices From The Lake (live) + more — Nowadays, Ridgewood. 10pm Saturday through 3pm Sunday. $20+; RSVP $10 before 11pm / $15 before midnight. Ends with Donato Dozzy & Neel live. RSVP (RA) · Venue facts
  • ABC Party (Anything But Clothes) — House of Yes, Bushwick. From ~$16. Costume-forward dance party. Tickets (Dice) · Venue facts

Sunday, Aug 30

  • Mister Sunday: Eamon Harkin All Day — Nowadays backyard, Ridgewood. 3–9pm. $10 off before 4pm / $5 off before 5pm with RSVP; Nonstop attendees free. The beloved outdoor Sunday ritual. RSVP (RA)
  • The Nursery: Leon Vynehall All Day Long — Public Records, Gowanus. Open-to-close in the garden; tickets on RA. Details (RA) · Venue facts

Free all weekend

Charlie Parker Jazz Festival — Marcus Garvey Park (Harlem) and Tompkins Square Park (East Village), Fri–Sun, free. Jazz at Lincoln Center Orchestra, Joshua Redman and Ravi Coltrane close SummerStage's 40th season. Perfect pre-game before an East Village bar run — try Death & Co or PDT after.

Skip this (the honest item)

Dirty Circus at House of Yes, Saturday — from $43. It's a well-produced burlesque/aerial variety show, but it's the priciest ticket in the building by a wide margin: the same venue's ABC Party on the same night starts at ~$16, Friday's SLAYYYLAND is $17, and $43 nearly matches a full day-and-night at Knockdown Center with Joe Claussell ($33). If you want House of Yes spectacle on a budget, the sub-$20 parties in the same rooms are the better value.

Plan it properly

  • Budget bands: Lean ($60–$120): Charlie Parker Fest free + one $16–$17 House of Yes night. Balanced ($120–$250): Dust Til Dawn + Bushwick afters. Premium ($250+): table at a rooftop, then Knockdown Center.
  • Buy presale. Every ticketed pick above rises in price as tiers sell — Saturday's big rooms routinely sell out.
  • Sequence by geography: Bushwick/Ridgewood/Maspeth cluster (Knockdown, Nowadays, House of Yes, Bossa Nova) — don't plan a Manhattan-Brooklyn double-jump after midnight.
