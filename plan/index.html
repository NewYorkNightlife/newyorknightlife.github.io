FAQ: NYC nightlife planning

What is the cheapest way to get around NYC at night?
For most routes, subway plus short walks is the lowest-cost option. Use rideshare strategically for late transfers, long cross-borough jumps, or when service frequency drops.
Is it better to bar-hop between neighborhoods in one night?
Usually no. Staying within one neighborhood cluster saves time, avoids surge pricing, and gives you more venue time.
When should I leave one venue for the next?
Set a hard departure time 30–45 minutes before your ideal next arrival. That buffer protects your plan against line delays, train waits, and traffic spikes.
